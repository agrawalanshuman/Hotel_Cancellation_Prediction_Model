# ISOM835_Hotel_Cancellation_Project
Suffolk University · ISOM 835 · Fall 2026

Anshuman Agrawal · Mark Belov · Phuc Hoang · Sarah Legault

Predict cancellation risk for each booking to support expected cancellations, overbooking and deposit policies. ROC-AUC measures ranking; Brier score, log loss and reliability plots assess whether probabilities support expected counts. Risk predictions alone do not justify causal deposit-policy changes.

Data: authorized competition training (83,573), test (35,817), sample submission. External datasets prohibited. Provisional prediction moment: booking creation, pending instructor confirmation. Even seemingly reasonable fields require timing review. The user confirmed instructor approval for four members; see TEAM_COMPLIANCE.md.

AI assistance: OpenAI Codex was used for coding, project organization, debugging, analysis support, and drafting. Team review is pending. Disclose ChatGPT separately if used; its use is not established here.
